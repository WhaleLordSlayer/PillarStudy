⚡ Quick Start for Editors

          
This internal QA viewer allows editors to explore, verify, and deep-link canonical relationships across the Bible. All graph data is read-only and reflects certified BGV2 checkpoints.

          

            
Search / Seed: Press / or click search to seed any Person, Place, Group, or Event.

            
Share: Click Share 🔗 to copy a direct link to the exact centered entity and hop depth.

            
Inspect: Click/tap any node or relationship line to view scripture citations, evidence, and participants.

            
Navigate: Scroll/pinch to zoom, drag canvas to pan, or drag any node to reposition it freely.

          

        


        
        

          
🔷 Node Shapes & Entity Types

          

            

              

                
                
Person (3,131)

              

              
Individual biblical persons with genealogical kinship (parents, children, spouses, siblings) and historical event participation.

            

            

              

                
                
Event (1,667)

              

              
Chronological biblical narrative episodes from BSB (1,597) and DSSU (70) sources, mapped with scripture ranges and participant links.

            

            

              

                
                
Place (1,001)

              

              
Geographic locations, cities, mountains, rivers, and regions where biblical events occurred.

            

            

              

                
                
Group (101)

              

              
Biblical tribes, factions, nationalities, and institutions (e.g., Israelites, Philistines, Pharisees, Disciples).

            

          

        


        
        

          
🔗 Relationship Lines & Edge Arrows

          

            

              

                
                
Canonical Kinship

Event Participation

              

              
Blue solid line: Connects individuals to historical events (Person → Event), events to places (Event → Place), or events to groups.

Controls visibility of inter-neighbor connections not directly incident to the focus node.

            

          

        


        
        

          
🎯 Hop Distance & Graph Adjacency

          
Hop traversal represents undirected graph distance:

          
    
            
  • 1 Hop (Default): Shows the center entity and all directly incident neighbors (kinship family, participated events, places).
    • 
            
  • 2 Hops: Expands to the neighbors of your neighbors (e.g. grandchildren, event co-participants, adjacent locations).
    • 
            
  • 3 Hops: Broad contextual graph expansion across biblical narrative clusters.
    •
