The Cultivate Data Project

The app is only the first thing built on this graph.

Explorer, Who’s Speaking, narrative context, and entity relationships depend on an open, verified scripture data layer. That foundation is built around traceable sources so useful relationships can be inspected with confidence.

We make our research and data models open for developers, educators, and the wider study community.

01 Connect the sources.

Relationships point back directly to scripture locators and verified claims.

02 Preserve the provenance.

The reasoning behind a connection matters just as much as the connection itself.

03 Let readers study.

The data layer supplies structure without replacing your own spiritual discovery.

Building scripture-study infrastructure — and a polished experience that shows what it makes possible.
