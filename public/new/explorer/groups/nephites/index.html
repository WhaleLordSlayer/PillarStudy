Nephites

Named in 80 chapters, from 2 Nephi 29 to Moroni 8.

Nephites were a people descended from Manasseh, the son of Joseph, and named after Nephi, the fourth son of Lehi. They occupied the American continent from around 590 B.C. to 385 A.D., when they were destroyed by the Lamanites. Originally descended from Nephi, Sam, Jacob, Joseph, and Zoram, the distinction between Nephites and Lamanites became more about religion and government than ancestry over time.

From George Reynolds' Dictionary of the Book of Mormon (1891)
