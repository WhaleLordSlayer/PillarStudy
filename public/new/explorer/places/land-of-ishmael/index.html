Land of Ishmael

Named in 7 chapters, from Alma 17 to Alma 25.

Ishmael, Land of was visited by Ammon, a missionary prince, in 91 B.C. It was ruled by King Lamoni and located down from the land of Nephi (Lehi-Nephi). The land was named after the sons of Ishmael, from whom the ruling dynasty descended. A highway connected Ishmael to Nephi and to the land of Middoni, as evidenced by King Lamoni and his father traveling along it. This suggests the land was situated in the alluvial plains east of the Andes, not in the strip of wilderness between the mountains and the Pacific Ocean.

From George Reynolds' Dictionary of the Book of Mormon (1891)
