Land of Bountiful (New World)

Named in 7 chapters, from 1 Nephi 17 to 3 Nephi 11.

Bountiful, Land of was a fertile region in Arabia where Nephi built the ship that carried Lehi's colony to the promised land. It was part of Arabia Felix, known for its abundance and productivity, and located on the shore of the Arabian Sea, which the people called Irreantum. This area should not be confused with another Bountiful in South America, where the Savior appeared to the Nephites.

From George Reynolds' Dictionary of the Book of Mormon (1891)

Includes George Reynolds' historical geographic interpretation.
