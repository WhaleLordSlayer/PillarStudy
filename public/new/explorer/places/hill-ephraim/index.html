Hill Ephraim

Named in 4 chapters, from 2 Nephi 17 to Ether 7.

Ephraim, Hill was a location in Central America from which Shule obtained iron ore to make swords and arm his followers in an effort to replace his father Kib on the throne. The hill was situated at no great distance from the land Moron, afterwards Desolation.

From George Reynolds' Dictionary of the Book of Mormon (1891)

Includes George Reynolds' historical geographic interpretation.
