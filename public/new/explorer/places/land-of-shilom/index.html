Land of Shilom

Named in 7 chapters, from Mosiah 7 to Alma 23.

Shilom, Land of was a small district, likely a valley, located near the land of Lehi-Nephi and on the direct route from Zarahemla to Lehi-Nephi. Zeniff and his colony, as well as Ammon and his party, traveled through it. Limhi and his people took a circuitous route around it to avoid detection. A prominent hill north of Shilom was a notable landmark, used by Nephites before they left under Mosiah I. King Noah later built a high tower there. After the Lamanites reoccupied the land, Amulon and his priests became teachers of its people. Eventually, the four sons of king Mosiah converted all the inhabitants of Shilom to the Lord.

From George Reynolds' Dictionary of the Book of Mormon (1891)
