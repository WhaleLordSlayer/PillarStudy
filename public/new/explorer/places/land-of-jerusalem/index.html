Land of Jerusalem

Named in 30 chapters, from 1 Nephi 2 to Ether 13.

Jerusalem, Land of was a land of the Lamanites located in the immediate vicinity of the waters of Mormon, probably east or north from Lehi-Nephi. Around 100 B.C., the Lamanites, Amulonites, and various Nephite apostates built a large and thriving city named after their fathers' ancient home in Judea. Aaron, the son of king Mosiah, preached the gospel there, but the city's citizens were too hardened in sin to accept his message. The city was later engulfed in the bowels of the earth during the calamities that followed the crucifixion of the Lord of Life and Glory. Waters rose in its place, and a stagnant sea replaced its grand palaces and synagogues.

From George Reynolds' Dictionary of the Book of Mormon (1891)
