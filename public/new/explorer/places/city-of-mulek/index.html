City of Mulek

Named in 7 chapters, from Mosiah 25 to Helaman 8.

Mulek, City of was a Nephite city located on the east coast, near the southern part of South America, about a day's journey south of Bountiful. It was captured by the Lamanites under Amalickiah in 67 B.C., with a Zoramite named Jacob placed in command. Moroni, Lehi, and Teancum later recaptured it in 64 B.C., and Moroni established it as Lehi's headquarters. The city fell again to the Lamanites in 34–33 B.C., but was retaken by Moronihah in 32 B.C. Later, Lehi and Nephi, sons of Helaman, visited the city during a mission to call the people to repentance, traveling from Bountiful to Lehi-Nephi in 30 B.C.

From George Reynolds' Dictionary of the Book of Mormon (1891)
