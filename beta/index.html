What you'll be trying

Three parts of Cultivate are at the center of the experience.

You can use the app however you normally study. These are the three areas most worth exploring first.

01 · Reader

One verse at a time.

Swipe through scripture one verse at a time for a focused reading flow, then switch to a traditional full chapter whenever you want more context.

02 · Study Spaces

Study together across devices.

Create or join a private group, follow shared reading progress, and share selected thoughts. Groups work across iOS and Android phones and tablets, while personal notes stay private unless you choose to share them.

03 · Cultivate Labs

Explore the people, stories, and topics behind the text.

Scripture Explorer connects people, places, events, groups, and source passages. The Topical Guide provides a topic-first path into related scripture across the standard works.

What beta testing means

Use it like the finished app.

The goal is not to make you follow a test script. Read, create a group, make notes, explore scripture, and see what feels natural. The testing phase is where we find the rough edges before a wider release.

Updates may arrive often.

New test builds are delivered through TestFlight on iOS and Google Play on Android.

You may hit a bug.

Something occasionally being incomplete or behaving unexpectedly is normal during this phase.

Confusing counts too.

If you cannot tell what a button does, where to find something, or what a feature means, that is just as useful to hear about as a crash.

Install Cultivate

Choose iOS or sign up for Android.

You will not find this test build by searching the public App Store or Google Play Store. Use the invitation link for your platform.

iOS

Install with Apple TestFlight

TestFlight is Apple's official app-testing system. It handles the Cultivate installation and future beta updates.

  1. Tap Join iOS test below.
  2. If TestFlight is not already installed, Apple will direct you to install it.
  3. Return to the Cultivate invitation in TestFlight, then tap Accept and Install.
  4. Future Cultivate test builds will update through TestFlight.
Join iOS test
Android

Install with Google Play testing

Google Play internal testing requires your Google account to be added before the install link will work. Start with the short signup form below. Once your account is added, you can join the test and install Cultivate through Google Play.

  1. Tap Sign up for Android below.
  2. Submit the Google account you use on your Android device.
  3. After your account is added to the testing list, use the Google Play invitation you receive to join the test and install Cultivate.
  4. Future test builds update through Google Play like a normal app.
