Account & privacy

Delete Your Cultivate Account

You can delete your Cultivate account and the personal data associated with it. The easiest way is directly from the Cultivate app. If you no longer have access to the app, you can request deletion by email.

Delete your account in the app

To delete your Cultivate account:

  1. Open Cultivate.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Go to Account.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have access to Cultivate, email Cultivate Support at cultivatestudy@gmail.com and ask us to delete your account.

Include the email address associated with your Cultivate account. We may need to verify that you own the account before completing the request.

Email Cultivate Support

What is deleted

Deleting your Cultivate account removes your account and personal data associated with it, including your profile and synced personal study data.

This may include:

  • notes
  • highlights
  • reading-plan progress
  • preferences and settings
  • Study Space membership and account information
  • other user-created or account-linked data

Some information may be retained where necessary to maintain shared content, comply with legal obligations, prevent abuse, or meet other legitimate operational requirements, as described in our Privacy Policy.
