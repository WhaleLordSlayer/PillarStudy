Built for trust

The connections should hold up when you follow them.

Explorer is designed around source passages and careful review so the map helps you study scripture instead of asking you to trust a black box.

Connections point back to scripture.

The goal is to help you reach the source passage quickly and see the connection in context.

Names and relationships are checked carefully.

Repeated names, quoted people, and related accounts are reviewed so similar-looking records are not casually treated as the same thing.

Uncertainty can stay uncertain.

When the evidence is not strong enough, Explorer can leave a relationship unresolved rather than fill the gap with a guess.

Where AI helps

AI is useful for finding candidates and stress-testing the data. Published Explorer connections are reviewed and packaged ahead of time. The app is not generating new relationships live while you read.
