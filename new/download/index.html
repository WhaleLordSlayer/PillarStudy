Before you start

A few beta notes.

What does beta mean?

You are trying Cultivate before wider release. Features may change and updates arrive through TestFlight or Google Play.

Can I study offline?

Scripture reading, packaged Explorer data, the Topical Guide, and personal lesson building remain useful offline. Sync, sharing, group updates, and Live require a connection.

How do I request Live Early Access?

Open Cultivate, start a Live Session, and complete the in-app request form when prompted.
