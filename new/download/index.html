A few things before you start.

What does beta mean?

You’re trying Cultivate before its wider release. Features may change, and you may encounter rough edges. Updates arrive through TestFlight or Google Play.

Can I study offline?

Scripture reading, packaged Explorer data, Topical Guide, and personal lesson building work offline. Sharing, cloud sync, group updates, and live lessons require a connection.

Does Cultivate use AI in the app?

No. Reading and discovery use scripture and packaged data. The app does not generate scripture commentary.

Are my personal notes shared?

Your notes stay private until you choose to share them. Private teacher notes are kept out of the shared lesson.
