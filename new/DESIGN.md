# Cultivate /new/ design direction

## Current homepage — October 7, 2026

The user-provided Stitch export is the visual reference. `/new/index.html` now loads reusable React/TypeScript components from `home.tsx` and `components.tsx`, with its own `editorial.css` entry. The current homepage uses cream (#fbf9f5), sage (#3e5a44 / #27422e), terracotta (#9b6241), warm ink (#1c1b19), Literata, and self-hosted DM Sans. Study / Together / Teach, a centered three-device hero, alternating experience sections, a six-step workflow, quieter feature depth, and the final download CTA are the central hierarchy.

Conceptual views are explicitly authorized for this pass and labelled as such. Product claims follow Cultivate source material, not Stitch's invented UI. Shared participation and Live are labelled in development. The actual Cultivate logo is retained. Production and existing deep pages stay intact. See STITCH-REVIEW.md for the current file list, QA, visual deviations, placeholders, and PM review items.

## Previous iteration — retained for existing detail-page context

The homepage centers on connecting families, friends, and classes through study groups and shared lessons, followed closely by deep, offline scripture discovery with no AI in the app. “Closer to scripture. Closer together.” leads the hero with a conceptual illustration of people connected around scripture. Study Spaces is the default feature-chooser path, followed by Shared lessons, Explorer, Reader, and Topical Guide. The same order carries through the navigation and homepage sections.

Lesson Builder is a supporting tool. Its screenshot is absent from the homepage; the shared-lesson section shows an actual finished lesson and emphasizes joining, following live, and returning asynchronously. The Lessons page leads with sharing and Live, then presents authoring later.

Following the owner's palette feedback, all nine routes use the current main site's navy (#07111f), teal (#10c6b4), warm paper (#f5f2eb), and quieter sage/neutral surfaces. Hero and dark sections use navy with pale mint accents; calls to action use the main site's teal gradient. Fonts and screenshots remain self-hosted.

Botanical SVGs and the overlapping Together circles are decorative marketing illustrations, not simulated app screens. Product screenshots remain the actual validated exports. Pending product captures stay labelled on the relevant detail pages.

Branding uses the actual circle-and-leaves logo copied from the main site's SVG, with its original teal gradient and unmodified vector path. It appears in the header and footer on all nine pages, and assets/cultivate-logo.svg supplies the /new/ favicon. The main site's assets remain unchanged.

The homepage feature chooser supports click, Tab, arrow keys, Home, and End with roving focus and a labelled tab panel. Without JavaScript, Study Spaces remains visible and every feature is available through navigation and the five feature sections. There is no automatic cycling. Reduced-motion preferences disable smooth scrolling and control transitions.
