Give your teaching a home.

The difference

One lesson is a moment.
A channel is continuity.

Use a direct lesson when you only need to share once. Use a Channel when the same people will keep coming back.

1

Build

Create the lesson from scripture, questions, notes, and saved study.

2

Publish

Add it to the Channel your class or family already knows.

3

Return

Participants come back for the next lesson without starting over.

What a Channel can become

A home for the groups you teach again.

Seminary

A persistent class channel with new lessons throughout the course. Students know where to return every time.

Sunday School

Share lessons around Sunday and keep participation connected from one week to the next.

Family Home Evening

Give an extended family one place for recurring lessons even when everyone is spread across different homes.

Async or Live

The Channel stays.
The teaching mode can change.

Participants can work through lessons on their own schedule, and Live Classes can bring everyone together when a synchronous session makes sense. Live is currently invite-only Early Access.
