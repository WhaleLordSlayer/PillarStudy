A modern interaction without losing serious study tools.

The scroller is not about making scripture disposable. It is about removing visual clutter and giving each verse a focused moment, especially on the device people already read from most.

  • Verse-by-verse scrolling designed for phones
  • Traditional reading mode when you want the chapter view
  • Highlights, notes, bookmarks, and your own scripture links
  • Story context and Explorer data stay connected to the verse

The product rule

Cultivate connects the data. You study the scripture.

Explorer is designed to expose traceable relationships and source passages, not generate an interpretation of what a verse means. Follow the connection, open the scripture, and study it for yourself.

The Cultivate data project

The app is only the first thing being built on top of the data.

Explorer, Who’s Speaking, narrative context, related passages, people, places, events, and relationships depend on a structured scripture data layer that has been a major project in its own right.

That foundation is being built around traceable sources and provenance, so useful relationships can be inspected instead of appearing as unexplained generated claims.

Over time, the goal is to make portions of that work available outside Cultivate so developers, educators, researchers, and the wider scripture-study community can build on it too.

01Connect the sources.

Relationships should point back to evidence in scripture or a documented source.

02Preserve the provenance.

The source behind a connection matters just as much as the connection itself.

03Let people study.

The data layer supplies context and connections. It does not replace scripture study with generated interpretation.

Building scripture-study infrastructure — and a polished app that shows what it can make possible.
