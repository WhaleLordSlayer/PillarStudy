05
Platforms

Use Cultivate across your devices.

Cultivate supports iOS and Android phones and tablets, keeps supported study data in sync, and includes a lightweight Apple Watch experience. Apple TV is planned.

iOS · Android · Tablets

Read and study on phones and tablets.

The full reader, Explorer, Study Spaces, notes, highlights, plans, and progress are available on iOS and Android. Tablet layouts use the larger screen for reading and study tools.

Apple Watch

Keep up with reading plans from your wrist.

Use Apple Watch for quick reading, plan progress, and a lightweight version of the core reading experience.

Apple TV · Roadmap

Apple TV is planned for shared study.

The roadmap includes a living-room version for reading plans, scripture, and Explorer content designed for a TV screen.

Other study tools

Topical Guide, Mastery, and Progress.

These tools cover three different needs: finding related passages, memorizing verses, and keeping track of reading plans.

Topical Guide

Browse topics and open the related verses.

Choose a topic to see scripture references connected to it. Open any reference in the reader, then move between the topic and the text as you study.

Mastery

Practice verses you want to memorize.

Add a verse to Mastery and revisit it in short recall sessions. Practice is separate from normal reading, so you can work on memorization without changing your reading plan.

Progress

Track plans and resume where you left off.

See what you have finished, what is next, and how your reading is progressing. Synced progress means you can start on one device and continue on another.

Family & friends beta

Family and friends testing starts this week.

Cultivate is being shared with a small group on iOS and Android before a wider beta. Feedback from this round will be used to fix rough edges and make sure the reader, Explorer, and Study Spaces are ready for more people.
