Study together without making everything public.

Your people. Your pace. Your private notes.

Built differently

A study tool should earn your trust.

01 · PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

Your personal study is yours.

Notes and private study content are not exposed to a Study Space just because you choose to share one thought.

02 · CONNECTIONS, NOT ANSWERS

Explore the evidence yourself.

Cultivate is designed to connect scripture, context, and study data — not to replace your interpretation of the text.

03 · INDEPENDENT

Built as a project worth supporting.

Cultivate Study is independently developed and is not an official product of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.
