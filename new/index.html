02
Plans that survive real life

Tell Cultivate what you want to study. It keeps the next step current.

Plans can be open-ended, paced to a finish date, weekly, or built from specific assignments. The point is to make today's reading obvious without turning an interrupted week into a pile of debt.

Free Read

Structure without a deadline.

Choose what you want to read and keep visible progress without a finish date or daily requirement.

Adjust your pace

Change the schedule, keep the work.

Reset or adjust a paced plan without throwing away the verses you already completed.

Linked progress

Read once. Let it count where it belongs.

When eligible plans overlap, observed reading can count across the exact shared verse ranges instead of making you maintain duplicate checklists.

03
Keep what matters

Your study stays connected to the scripture it came from.

Save a verse in the moment, build on it later, and return to it without turning your study into one long notes document.

Saved

Bookmarks, notes, highlights, collections.

Keep verses, highlight specific word ranges, add notes and tags, organize collections, and share from the same saved material later.

Book of Mormon Story Map

See the shape of the chapter while you read.

All 239 Book of Mormon chapters include reviewed chapter orientation with a concise summary and narrative story beats. Speaker labels help make shifts in voice easier to follow.

Scripture Search

Search the text and jump straight to the verse.

Full-text scripture search runs against the local scripture database with source filters and direct navigation into the reader.

Build memory. Build rhythm.

Progress that helps you return instead of punishing you for leaving.

Cultivate separates memorization practice from reading progress and treats consistency as something that can grow again after a missed day.

Mastery

Memorize the words and learn where they live.

Create a Mastery set for passages you want to remember. Practice moves through increasingly demanding recall, including whole-passage work for multi-verse selections.

Momentum

Build rhythm, not a fragile streak.

Momentum looks at recent study consistency. Missing a day lowers the score instead of wiping your progress back to zero.

Progress

See what you finished and what comes next.

Verse-level plan progress, reading history, recent rhythm, milestones, and continuation help turn a broad goal into a concrete next step.

04
Go deeper

Two discovery systems built around the scripture itself.

Explorer follows the story through connected people, places, events, and groups. Topical Guide follows ideas across passages and vocabulary.

Scripture Explorer

The Bible graph alone includes 3,131 people, 1,667 events, and 11,875 relationships.

Follow a person into events, places, groups, and source passages across the Book of Mormon, Old Testament, New Testament, and Pearl of Great Price.

Topical Guide

304 topics. 14,707 accepted links. 8,554 unique verses reached.

Related Verses

Follow a connection without losing your place.

06
Use Cultivate where you study

Phone, tablet, and wrist, with your study context carried between them.

The core reading and study experience is built for iOS and Android phones and tablets, with account-backed continuity and a focused Apple Watch companion.

iOS + Android

Full study on phones and tablets.

Reader, plans, Saved, Mastery, Progress, Study Spaces, Explorer, and Topical Guide are designed around the main mobile experience, with responsive tablet layouts.

Apple Watch

Keep the next reading close.

Continue reading, check plans and progress, and record lightweight reading activity without rebuilding the whole phone app on your wrist.

Reader preferences
