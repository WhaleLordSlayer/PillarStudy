Built for real groups

Same scriptures. Different rhythms.

Study Spaces work whether everyone lives together, meets once a week, or is spread across time zones.

Family study

Share a plan and keep scripture study moving even when schedules do not line up perfectly.

Friends or small groups

Follow a reading plan together, share thoughts, and encourage consistency without making it competitive.

Classes

Keep study and participation going between meetings instead of restarting from zero every week.

Extended family

Give family in different cities, states, or countries a place to study together on their own time.

Sunday groups

Carry scripture-linked thoughts and lessons beyond a single meeting.

Shared plans

Keep a common direction while allowing each person to read at a pace that fits their life.

When study becomes teaching

Study Spaces connect naturally to Lessons.

A group can keep studying together, while recurring teaching can live in a Lesson Channel. Cultivate separates those ideas so each one has a clear purpose, then connects them when your group needs both.
