Sharing

One lesson.
The right people.

Publish a shared copy and invite someone with its link or code. For an ongoing family or class, a Lesson Channel keeps shared lessons together.

Live Lessons

Your lesson.
Everyone on the same page.

Move through the shared lesson while participant devices follow. Your teaching notes stay on your host view; the group sees the shared content.

Live or on their own time.

The shared lesson can stay available after the live session. Participants can return and work through it at their own pace. Sharing and live sessions need a connection.
