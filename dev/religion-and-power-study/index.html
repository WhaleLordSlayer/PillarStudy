THE QUESTION01

What can scripture help us see when religious identity, national belonging, and political power begin to reinforce one another?

This study follows a modern argument through a structured scripture corpus. It looks for recurring questions about status, chosenness, outsiders, mercy, truth, and the uses of authority—not to make ancient groups identical to modern ones, but to let the texts put pressure on the claims being examined.
