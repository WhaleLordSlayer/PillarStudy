03
Scripture Explorer

See the story behind the verse.

Explorer turns scripture into a connected map of people, places, events, groups, speakers, story moments, and source passages. Start with one record and keep following the connections.

People

Who is connected to whom?

Open a person and follow the people, events, places, groups, and source passages around them.

Story

What happened, where, and who was there?

Events connect participants, places, groups, and the scripture passages that describe them.

Scale

3,131 people. 1,667 events. 11,875 Bible relationships.

And that is only the Bible layer. Explorer also covers the Book of Mormon and Pearl of Great Price.

04
More ways to study

Powerful tools, ready when you want them.

The reader, groups, and Explorer are the center of Cultivate. Around them are deeper tools for topical discovery, planning, memorization, personal study, search, and progress.

Mastery

Practice the passages you want to carry with you.

Build a Mastery set and work from guided recall toward whole-passage memory. It is a smaller part of Cultivate, but a deep one when memorization is your goal.

Topical Guide

Explore scripture by topic and meaning.

Browse 304 accepted topics and follow more than 14,000 accepted topic-to-verse relationships across the standard works.

See Topical Guide
Plans

Know what to read next without feeling behind.

Use open-ended Free Read, paced goals, weekly reading, or custom assignments. Adjust the schedule later without throwing away completed reading.

Personal study

Save it and come back to it.

Bookmarks, highlights, notes, collections, Momentum, scripture search, and reading history keep your study useful after the moment passes.

Across your devices

Keep your study context with you.

Cultivate supports iOS and Android phones and tablets with account-backed sync. A focused Apple Watch companion keeps continuation and reading-plan progress close at hand.

Family & friends beta

Family and friends testing starts this week.

The first beta round is focused on the three experiences at the center of Cultivate: the reader, Study Spaces, and Explorer, along with the sync and study tools that support them.
